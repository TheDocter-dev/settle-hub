# Settle External Reviewer Seat — Role and Terms

**Status: DRAFT v2 for 22 Sep 2026 launch package — seat published in dated form matching the independence policy**

## Purpose

The external reviewer is Settle's standing hostile reader. Once staffed, every pre-registration, method-version change, and draft publication passes the reviewer before it ships. The seat exists so that Settle's rigour does not depend on the founder's self-criticism alone.

## Scope of review

- **Pre-registrations** — checked for testability: invariants over named entities and bounded conditions, never totals over open sets.
- **Method versions** — checked for correctness of the change, honest treatment of prior versions' output, and hash-gating of the deployed artifact.
- **Draft publications** — checked for denominator discipline (every share states its base), claim-evidence match, and leak-rule compliance (public-or-anchored references only).
- **The audit trail itself** — spot-checked: state-hash continuity, deploy-log completeness, redaction policy compliance.

## Terms

- **Independence:** the reviewer is not paid by, invested in, or employed by any party Settle measures, or by a competing verification service. The founder cannot silently overrule the reviewer — overruling is possible only for uncommissioned publications, and only with verbatim disclosure (see Publication and veto).
- **Access:** the reviewer sees internal artifacts in full under confidentiality — including address-level mappings; the redaction policy (S.1) governs what *readers* see, not what the reviewer sees (an audit-trail spot-check is impossible without the mappings). Confidentiality terms never restrict the reviewer from publishing a disagreement.
- **Publication and veto:** for **commissioned assessments**, a reviewer block is binding — the report does not ship until the disagreement is resolved. For **uncommissioned publications**, the founder may ship over a disagreement only with the disagreement disclosed verbatim in the methods section. The reviewer's rulings are summarized in each publication's methods section.
- **Compensation and term:** agreed on appointment and disclosed on this page. Either side may end the arrangement with notice, and the seat's vacancy is disclosed on the independence page within 7 days.

## Seat status

**Vacant at launch. The seat will be filled before Settle issues its first commissioned assessment; until it is filled, no commissioned assessment is issued.**

*Until staffed, drafts pass Settle's internal adversarial review (AI-assisted, described on the About page), with rulings recorded in the project handoff log. This is internal quality control and is not represented as external review anywhere.*
