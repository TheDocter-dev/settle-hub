# Milestone 01 — First assessment published: Faremeter v0.22.0 (22 September 2026)

Settle is an independent, protocol-level conformance and measurement lab for the x402 agent-payment protocol. Today we publish our first assessment: **a self-hosted Faremeter v0.22.0 on Base Sepolia, assessed as a method demonstration.**

Every claim in the assessment resolves to a Base Sepolia transaction or a hash-committed artifact listed in its [evidence manifest](https://github.com/TheDocter-dev/settle-hub/blob/main/assessments/001-artifact-hashes.txt); the catalog's method and code are [published alongside](https://github.com/TheDocter-dev/settle-hub/blob/main/chain-catalog-v3.py). The assessment, its evidence manifest, and every governing document are hash-anchored in the Settle Ledger ([anchors/001](https://github.com/TheDocter-dev/settle-ledger/tree/main/anchors/001) and corrective [anchor 001a](https://github.com/TheDocter-dev/settle-ledger/tree/main/anchors/001a), OpenTimestamps Bitcoin proofs). Full artifact list with hashes: [anchors/001a/SHA256SUMS](https://github.com/TheDocter-dev/settle-ledger/blob/main/anchors/001a/SHA256SUMS).

The full draft and raw artifacts were shared privately with the maintainers on 15 September under our [disclosure policy](https://github.com/TheDocter-dev/settle-hub/blob/main/disclosure-policy.md); no response was received, and the report publishes on the stated date. The maintainers did not participate and have not reviewed it. The report characterizes only v0.22.0 on Base Sepolia and makes no claim about other versions or networks. Faremeter builds in the open, which is what made an independent assessment possible.

The x402 verification space now includes academic security studies (Wang et al., USENIX Security 2026) and public liveness dashboards; Settle's contribution is continuous, per-version conformance assessment of individual implementations, each report reproducible from on-chain evidence under a published disclosure policy.

Also publishing today: the [independence policy](https://github.com/TheDocter-dev/settle-hub/blob/main/independence-policy.md), [disclosure policy](https://github.com/TheDocter-dev/settle-hub/blob/main/disclosure-policy.md), [external-reviewer-seat terms](https://github.com/TheDocter-dev/settle-hub/blob/main/external-reviewer-seat.md), and a [mechanism inventory](https://github.com/TheDocter-dev/settle-hub/blob/main/MECHANISMS.md) that ties every operational claim we make to an implementation on record or a dated plan.
